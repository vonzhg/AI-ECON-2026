### 1.3 Lab 7A — Text as Data (**numpy-only, no torch**)

Embeddings via **co-occurrence + PPMI + truncated SVD** (deterministic, seconds on CPU) with a markdown aside tying it to word2vec/SGNS (Levy–Goldberg equivalence). Lecture notation mirrored throughout (`K: A* → {1..M}*`, `E: {1..M} → R^d`, `m(x_i)`).

- **Part 0**: title + lab philosophy + "🌐 offline-safe" note; env-check cell loads the bundled corpus and prints summary.
- **Part 1 — From text to tokens**: regex word tokenizer, vocab + token IDs; toy BPE (~50 merges learned on FOMC text) showing subword splits of "raising"/"unemployment". ✏️ **A1**: apply learned merges to an unseen word; compare vs word-level OOV.
- **Part 2 — BoW & TF-IDF**: reproduce the lecture's 2-doc worked example ("central bank cuts rates…"/"central bank raises rates…") and the N=100 IDF table **verbatim** (assert equality with the slide numbers); scale to full-corpus TF-IDF, top terms for 2008-12-16 / 2020-03-15 vs a quiet 2017 meeting. ✏️ **A2**: cosine of the two toy docs under counts vs TF-IDF.
- **Part 3 — Dictionary methods**: mini EPU-style uncertainty index over 1994–2025, plot with 2008/2020 annotations; ties to Baker–Bloom–Davis (consult dataviz skill for chart styling).
- **Part 4 — Embeddings from co-occurrence**: window ±5, top-5k vocab, PPMI → SVD d=100; nearest neighbors of `inflation`/`employment`; analogy attempt with honest small-corpus caveat; **hawk–dove direction** `v = mean(u_raise,u_increase,u_tighten) − mean(u_lower,u_cut,u_ease)` (synonym sets — verify token counts in-notebook), score statements, plot against known hiking/cutting cycles. ✏️ **A3**: restrict scoring to sentences containing "federal funds rate", compare noise.
- **Part 5 — Statement-change series**: δ_t = 1 − cos(TF-IDF(D_{t−1}), TF-IDF(D_t)); largest jumps should land on 2008-12/2020-03.
- Closers: `Make it your own`, `Wrap-up` (bridge: "counting can't model order — attention can"), `## Appendix` with A1–A3 solutions.

### 1.4 Lab 7B — Attention & a minimal GPT (**PyTorch**)

- **Part 0/env-check**: torch import, `torch.set_num_threads(min(4, os.cpu_count()))`, seeds, load corpus, print char-vocab size.
- **Part 1 — Attention in 5 steps (numpy)**: reproduce the lecture's 9-token sentence "The central bank raised interest rates amid rising inflation"; first hard-code the slide's scores for query token *interest* (rates 2.3, inflation 1.9, raised 1.1, bank 0.5, the −0.2) → softmax → α table matching the slide (α≈0.50/0.40/0.05); then the general 5 steps with lecture notation (Q/K/V, s_j^(i)=⟨Q_i,K_j⟩/√k, α, weighted sum, W_O) + α heatmap. ✏️ **B1**: implement `scaled_dot_attention(Q,K,V)`, check against a hand-computed 2-token case.
- **Part 2 — Causal mask, multi-head, entropy**: lower-triangular mask, H heads (k=d/H), attention entropy H_i = −Σ α log α diagnostic. ✏️ **B2**: implement `causal_mask(T)`, show before/after weights.
- **Part 3 — Minimal GPT**: char-level vocab (M<100), TokenEmbedding + learned positional embedding, pre-LN blocks (MHSA + 4× GELU MLP), final LN + lm_head; print param count; map every hyperparameter to lecture symbols d/k/H/L/n/M with the GPT-2 scale table for contrast.
- **Part 4 — Training** (target ≤10–15 min CPU): `d=128, H=4, L=3, n=128, batch=32, dropout=0.1`, AdamW lr=3e-4 (100-step warmup), `max_steps=3000`, held-out 5% eval every 250 steps, grad clip 1.0 → ≈0.63 M params, ≈8–13 min on 4 threads. `FAST_MODE=True` flag → 600 steps (~2 min smoke run). Loss curve + stated expectations (start ≈ ln M ≈ 4.5, end ≈ 1.3–1.6 nats/char). In-notebook `assert` final val loss < 2.0 and decreasing (so nbconvert fails loudly).
- **Part 5 — Generation**: temperature sampling {0.5, 0.8, 1.2} from prompt "The Committee decided"; learned-head attention-entropy heatmap revisit. ✏️ **B3**: implement top-k sampling (k=10).
- Closers: `Make it your own` (word-level with 7A's BPE vocab, scale L/d), `Wrap-up` (bridge to Lec08/RAG: the model memorizes style, not facts — retrieval fixes that), `## Appendix` with B1–B3 solutions.
