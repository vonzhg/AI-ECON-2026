# Example Questions

Use these in class to show how retrieval changes the answer.

## Core macro questions

- How do Spear and Young distinguish OLG from ILA models?
- Why was Samuelson 1958 important for monetary economics?
- What did Diamond 1965 add to the OLG approach?
- What role did Lucas 1972 play in the paper's history?
- Why do the authors argue ILA became dominant over OLG?
- How do the authors compare OLG, SOLG, ILA, and SILA?

## Retrieval diagnostics

- Find passages about Barro's reinterpretation of OLG.
- Find passages about stochastic OLG and recursive equilibrium.
- What does the paper say about textbook coverage of OLG?

- What does this paper say about the December 2025 FOMC meeting?
